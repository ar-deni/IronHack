My Favorite Characters

Arale

A robot hero from the manga and anime Dr. Slump, created by scientist Senbei Norimaki. Childlike, naive and very funny, she possesses superhuman strength and creates comic mischief.

Son Goku

The main character of Dragon Ball, a kind-hearted Saiyan who survived the destruction of his planet. Super strength, martial arts skills, and a pure heart make him Earth's defender and favorite of millions.

Monkey D. Luffy

A charismatic pirate captain dreaming of becoming the Pirate King. Luffy's special feature is his rubber body, thanks to the power of the "devil fruit", with inexhaustible optimism and a thirst for adventure.

Pantera Rosa

An icon of world animation, a charming and silent pink panther who often finds himself in funny and absurd situations. He is simultaneously smart and funny.

Bugs Bunny

A clever and funny rabbit from the Looney Tunes cartoons, who loves carrots and easily outsmarts his opponents with his wit.

Popeye

A sailor from classic American cartoons. He possesses super strength after eating spinach, is always ready to help friends, and protects his love Olive Oyl.
